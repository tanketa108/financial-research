# MSFT Deep Dive v0 — Scope

Status: in progress
Purpose: prepare a deep research pack for the user's own analysis, not a final thesis or recommendation.

## Core objective

Understand Microsoft as a business system:

- how it makes money,
- segment economics and growth,
- margin structure,
- working capital behavior,
- capital allocation and capex/FCF conversion,
- debt maturity and debt service capacity,
- business strengths/weaknesses,
- market/sector dynamics,
- guidance and management outlook,
- key open questions for the user's thesis work.

## Requested output style

The deep dive should be a source-backed research pack, not a polished investment memo.

It should separate:

1. Facts and sourced data.
2. Analytical interpretation.
3. Open questions / verification required.
4. Suggested next work.

## Sections

1. Executive map of the business model
2. Segment analysis
   - Productivity and Business Processes
   - Intelligent Cloud / Azure
   - More Personal Computing
   - AI/Copilot overlay
   - Gaming / LinkedIn / search where material
3. Revenue growth and margin analysis by segment
4. Working capital flow
5. Capital flow
   - capex
   - depreciation/amortization
   - FCF conversion
   - buybacks
   - dividends
   - SBC/dilution
6. Debt and liquidity
   - debt maturity schedule
   - interest expense / cost of debt
   - cash and investments
   - capacity to pay/refinance
7. Market and sector dynamics
   - cloud infrastructure
   - enterprise software
   - AI infrastructure and apps
   - productivity suites

8. Guidance and outlook
9. Strengths / weaknesses
10. Key thesis questions
11. Data gaps and next steps

1. Microsoft latest 10-K
2. Microsoft latest 10-Q
3. Microsoft earnings release / CFO commentary / investor relations
4. Earnings call transcript if available
5. SEC filings and company IR
6. Secondary sources only for context, labelled as such


## Addendum requested by user — profitability and working capital decomposition

Add two required analytical blocks to MSFT deep dive v0.1:

### ROE DuPont analysis

Purpose: understand where Microsoft’s return on equity comes from.

Include:

- ROE trend.
- Net profit margin.
- Asset turnover.
- Equity multiplier / leverage.
- Optional extended DuPont if data quality allows:
  - tax burden,
  - interest burden,
  - operating margin,
  - asset turnover,
  - financial leverage.
- Interpretation: whether high ROE is driven by margin quality, asset efficiency, leverage, buybacks/equity base shrinkage, or accounting effects.

### Working capital and cash conversion cycle

Purpose: understand cash conversion dynamics and operating funding model.

Include:

- DSO — days sales outstanding.
- DIO — days inventory outstanding.
- DPO — days payable outstanding.
- CCC — cash conversion cycle = DSO + DIO - DPO.
- Deferred revenue / contract liabilities as separate subscription/software funding dynamic.
- Receivables, inventory, payables and accrued expenses trends.
- Interpretation of whether working capital is a source or use of cash.
- Segment nuance: Microsoft inventory is less central than for industrials/retail; deferred revenue and enterprise billing are more important.
