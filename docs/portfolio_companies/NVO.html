Thesis State

Novo Nordisk is in research-pending status after a growth/price reset. Initial read: the company remains a GLP-1 leader with strong obesity/diabetes assets, but 2026 guidance embeds material pricing/competition pressure and lower US realised prices.

  • Owner: user
  • Last reviewed: 2026-05-17
  • Portfolio Manager decision: [missing]

What would change our mind

  • Evidence that US realised price pressure is worse/better than current guidance implies.
  • Oral Wegovy uptake, persistence and channel mix differ materially from assumptions.
  • Lilly/tirzepatide competitive pressure changes Novo's volume share trajectory.
  • CagriSema, semaglutide 7.2 mg or other pipeline decisions materially alter long-term obesity share/margin outlook.

Business Snapshot

Global healthcare company focused on diabetes, obesity and rare disease, with core economics driven by GLP-1 therapies including Ozempic and Wegovy.

  • Segments: Obesity care, Diabetes care, Rare disease
  • Competitors: Eli Lilly, other GLP-1/obesity entrants
  • Cycle exposure: Healthcare/pharma; less macro-cyclical than industrials, but exposed to pricing, reimbursement, regulation, patent cycles and competitive clinical data.

Key Drivers

  • Global GLP-1 volume expansion in obesity and diabetes.
  • Wegovy and oral Wegovy uptake, access and channel mix.
  • Ozempic durability in diabetes despite market share pressure.
  • US realised prices, rebates, Medicaid/coverage and MFN effects.
  • Manufacturing capacity expansion and gross margin recovery.
  • Pipeline optionality: CagriSema, semaglutide 7.2 mg, zenagamtide/amycretin, cardiovascular/metabolic assets.

Key Risks

  • 2026 adjusted sales and operating profit guidance implies decline at CER due to lower realised prices and competition.
  • US Operations pressure from pricing, coverage changes and competition.
  • Global diabetes value share declined in 2025.
  • Gross margin pressure from Catalent-site depreciation/amortisation, restructuring and capacity expansion costs.
  • Patent/compound expiry impacts for semaglutide in certain International Operations markets.
  • Execution risk in scaling supply chain and launching oral Wegovy.

Watch Metrics

  • Obesity care sales growth and branded GLP-1 obesity volume share.
  • Wegovy prescription trends, including oral Wegovy launch metrics.
  • Ozempic sales growth and GLP-1 diabetes value share.
  • US vs International Operations sales growth.
  • Gross margin and operating margin bridge, including restructuring/Catalent effects.
  • Free cash flow after high CAPEX.
  • Management guidance revisions for adjusted sales/operating profit growth.

Open Questions

  • What is the normalised 2027-2030 revenue/margin trajectory after 2026 pricing reset?
  • How much oral Wegovy is incremental vs cannibalising injectable Wegovy/GLP-1 demand?
  • What share and price assumptions are required for Novo to defend premium valuation vs Lilly?
  • How should we adjust margins for restructuring, Catalent integration and capacity ramp costs?
  • What is the probability-weighted value of CagriSema, semaglutide 7.2 mg and next-generation assets?
