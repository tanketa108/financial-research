Current stance: under review

Risks

  • Commodity price weakness quickly eroding earnings and cash flow durability
  • Capital allocation becoming pro-cyclical or value-destructive
  • Apparent cheapness proving less attractive under normalized through-cycle assumptions

Catalysts

  • Sustained evidence that cash generation is being returned to shareholders with discipline
  • Balance-sheet resilience holding up through softer commodity conditions
