Current stance: under review

What changes my mind: Evidence that AI demand is failing to convert into durable margin-accretive growth, or that capital intensity rises faster than monetization.

Risks

  • AI capex intensity outpacing monetization and pressuring returns
  • Cloud growth decelerating faster than expected in core enterprise workloads
  • Regulatory or competitive pressure weakening Microsoft ecosystem pricing power

Catalysts

  • Sustained Azure growth with improving evidence of AI monetization
  • Operating margin durability despite elevated AI infrastructure spend
  • Continued capital allocation discipline with resilient free cash generation
